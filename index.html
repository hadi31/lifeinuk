Privacy Policy

Life in the UK Test 2027 · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Unofficial practice app. Not affiliated with the UK Home Office.

Summary

You do not need an account to use this app, and it does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. Your study progress never leaves your device.

What is stored on your device

Your answers, mock exam scores, saved questions and appearance setting are stored only on your device, so the app can show your progress. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

If you buy the full version, the payment is handled by the app store you downloaded the app from. The app never sees your payment details. To check and restore purchases the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random app user identifier, your purchase history for this app and basic device information such as country and operating system version. It does not receive your name, email address or study progress.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. The only outside service it uses is RevenueCat, described under Purchases.

Links to other websites

The app links to GOV.UK so you can read about and book the official test. When you open a link you leave the app, and the privacy policy of that website applies.

Delete your data

To ask for your data from Life in the UK Test 2027 to be deleted, email Hadi31 Apps, the developer, at hadi31.apps@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”. Within 30 days we will delete your RevenueCat customer record, which holds your anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. Google Play keeps its own record of the purchase, so you can still restore the full version afterwards. Your progress data is stored only on your device and never reaches us: uninstalling the app deletes it, and so does Reset progress in Settings.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the test and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hadi31.apps@gmail.com
